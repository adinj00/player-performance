## Brand & Style

This design system channels the heritage, fierce passion, and contemporary sporting culture of FK Velež Mostar. Built around the club's iconic identity ("Rođeni"), the aesthetic balances an aggressive, high-energy football club spirit with the polished functionality of a premier digital platform and retail merchandise experience.

The design style combines **Modern Athletic High-Contrast** with structured **Brutalist-leaning Sports Geometry**. The interface leverages deep slate-black canvases, electric white contrast, sharp athletic structural lines, and decisive strikes of authentic Velež crimson red (`#D3132C`) balanced by championship amber (`#FBBF24`). Visual storytelling evokes raw match-day energy, stadium architectural rhythm, and dedicated supporter loyalty. Interactions are rapid, punchy, and confident, emphasizing physical momentum through high-contrast status cues, dense typography, and razor-sharp framing.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around high visual friction and undeniable sporting presence, default-themed in a deep stadium-night aesthetic:

- **Primary (`#D3132C` - Club Crimson):** The soul of FK Velež. Used for primary calls-to-action, match live states, club badges, score highlights, and active supporter states.
- **Secondary (`#FBBF24` - Stadium Gold):** Trophies, referee cards, membership tiers, VIP callouts, kit badges, and highlight accents.
- **Tertiary (`#F1F5F9` - Terrace Light):** High-readability foreground copy, structural tabular numbers, and sharp contrast button fills against dark panels.
- **Neutral (`#0F172A` - Pitch Slate):** The bedrock background canvas. Supported by `pitch-slate-surface` (`#1E293B`) for cards and match sheets, framed by `pitch-slate-border` (`#334155`).

Color is deployed with deliberate hierarchy: the canvas remains anchored in deep pitch dark tones so that Crimson and Gold pop with explosive, stadium-floodlight intensity.

## Typography

The typographic system pairs the condensed, commanding power of **Oswald** with the hyper-legible, utilitarian clarity of **Fira Sans**.

- **Display & Headlines (Oswald):** Condensed and uppercase-biased for match scores, club anthems, player squad numbers, and section banners. Emulates physical stadium architecture, scoreboards, and kit typographic treatments.
- **Body & Editorial (Fira Sans):** Delivers clean editorial voice for match reports, club history, and e-commerce descriptions. Highly legible even against low-light slate backgrounds.
- **Labels, Badges, and Stats (Fira Sans - Medium/Bold with tracking):** Uppercase, letterspaced labels bring a disciplined, tactical aesthetic to match telemetry, merchandise sizing, timestamps, and league tables.

## Layout & Spacing

The system runs on a rigid, athletic **12-column fluid grid** system optimized for responsive content pacing across fixtures, rosters, and apparel grids:

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns with `margin: 2rem` (32px) and `gutter: 1.5rem` (24px). Section paddings use expansive vertical rhythms (`space-xl` or larger) to let bold typography breathe.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns with `margin: 1.5rem` (24px) and `gutter: 1rem` (16px).
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with `margin-sm: 1rem` (16px) and `gutter-sm: 1rem` (16px). Grids collapse into vertical stacks with tight, athletic touch density.

Component internal spacing adheres strictly to 4px/8px multiples (`0.25rem` to `2.5rem`), ensuring consistent horizontal lockups for score banners, timer bars, and product detail matrices.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids excessive, floaty soft shadows in favor of **Tonal Layering**, **Crisp Outlines**, and **Subtle Colored Glows**:

- **Base Ground (`pitch-slate` / `#0F172A`):** The default viewport canvas.
- **Container Tiers:**
  - **Level 1 (Surface Cards):** `#1E293B` bounded by a 1px border of `#334155`.
  - **Level 2 (Active/Hovered Containers):** `#253348` with a 1px border shifting to `club-crimson` (`#D3132C`) or `stadium-gold` (`#FBBF24`).
  - **Level 3 (Modals & Flyouts):** `#1E293B` overlaid with a sharp 2px border and a directional, ground-clamped shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`.
- **Match-Day Energy & Glows:** High-priority items (live match indicators, scoring alerts, supporter campaign buttons) emit a tight, colored ambient halo: `0 0 16px rgba(211, 19, 44, 0.4)`.

## Shapes

The shape grammar adheres to an aggressive, athletic **Soft/Structured Form (Level 1)**. Sports equipment, stadium turnstiles, and brutalist architecture rely on sturdy, authoritative edges:

- Standard controls, chips, input fields, and fixture boxes employ `0.25rem` (4px) radii.
- Medium components and modal frames step up to `0.5rem` (8px).
- Heavy pill rounding is prohibited except for small live tags (e.g., `LIVE`, `HT`, `FT`) which use fully rounded pills for contrast against the otherwise chiseled layout.
- Selected showcase cards utilize angled chamfers (12px diagonal cut-outs on top-right corners) on special edition merchandise and match-day fixtures to reinforce forward momentum.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Athletic (`.btn-primary`):** Solid Club Crimson (`#D3132C`) background, terrace-white text (`#FFFFFF`), Oswald medium/bold uppercase, 4px border radius. Hover: `#B00D22` with a subtle red glow. Active: slight 1px scale-down.
- **Secondary Supporter (`.btn-secondary`):** Transparent background with 1.5px border in `#F1F5F9`, white text. Hover: fills with white, text shifts to `#0F172A`.
- **Accent Championship (`.btn-accent`):** Solid Stadium Gold (`#FBBF24`), deep slate text (`#0F172A`), for VIP membership, seasonal passes, and jersey customization.

### Cards & Match Sheets
- **Fixture Card:** `#1E293B` background, 1px `#334155` border. Divided into three horizontal segments: top metadata (league & stadium in Oswald uppercase), center head-to-head match-up with team crests and Oswald score displays, and bottom action footer with ticket/stream buttons.
- **Merchandise Product Card:** Clean image showcase with pitch-slate surface, subtle red hover-stroke on border, sticky price pill in Oswald, and category badges top-left.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Chips:** High-contrast compact pills with uppercase Oswald or Fira Sans bold (`10px`).
  - *Live Match:* Deep crimson fill with flashing white dot.
  - *League Table Standing:* Slate fill with 1px gold border for European spots, slate border for mid-table.
- **Filter Chips:** Slate surface `#1E293B` with `#334155` border. Active state switches border and text to Club Crimson with subtle red tint background (`rgba(211, 19, 44, 0.15)`).

### Inputs & Forms
- **Input Fields:** Dark slate `#151E2E` surface, 1px `#334155` border, terrace-light text. Placeholders in `#94A3B8`.
- **Focus State:** 1.5px border transitions to `#D3132C` with no soft outer glow, maintaining a sharp, performance-engineered finish.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Square 18px boxes with 2px borders in `#334155`. Checked state fills with `#D3132C` displaying a bold white checkmark icon.

### Lists & League Tables
- Alternating dark slate row stripes (`#0F172A` / `#141E30`), bordered bottom by 1px `#1E293B`. Numbers, goals, and point totals aligned strictly via tabular Oswald figures to ensure clean scanning.
